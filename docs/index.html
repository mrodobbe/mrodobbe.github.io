Maarten Dobbelaere

Scientist · Laboratory for Artificial Chemical Intelligence (LIAC), EPFL

I am a scientist at the Laboratory for Artificial Chemical Intelligence (LIAC) at EPFL, working on AI for chemical synthesis planning. In May 2025, I obtained my PhD at Ghent University with the thesis "Molecular Machine Learning for Chemical Reaction Engineering", supervised by Kevin Van Geem and Christian Stevens.
